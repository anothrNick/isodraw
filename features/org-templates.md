# Organization templates

**Status:** idea · **Priority:** later
**Summary:** Save any diagram as a template for the team, shown beside the built-in ones when creating a diagram.

- *Save as template* on a diagram copies its content into the workspace's templates, with a name and description.
- The new-diagram dialog shows the workspace's templates first, then the built-in ones.
- Templates can be updated (saving again replaces) and removed by owners and admins.
